Generating software opportunities from features

After having picked features and processing them through internal rejection system, software opportunities are generated. Each software opportunity contains following information: 

+ Specification for product with unique brand name having feature as main one
+ Answer for "Are there any products implementing this feature?"
+ The specific customer segment
+ The painful workflow/problem
+ The feature customers pay for
+ Existing products charging money for similar functionality
+ Pricing customers already accept
+ Customer complaints or requests indicating unmet demand
+ Why existing solutions are insufficient
+ Estimated price customers could tolerate
+ Evidence that the problem occurs frequently
+ WHO pays?
+ WHY do they pay?
+ WHAT exactly do they pay for?
+ HOW MUCH do they pay?
+ HOW OFTEN does the problem occur?
+ WHAT are they dissatisfied with?
+ WHY isn't the existing solution good enough?
+ Rejection Testing Reports

About Us

Solve best features which users are willing to pay for.

Software businesses need to ensure that users are willing to and/or ready to pay for softwares' features before they can invest resources to build those features. The Build-Measure-Learn cycle has become popular. Which features will be next MVP? Is it worth to build it? Ceptize are trying to solve this problem. Ceptize are extracting "willing to pay" features from most interested products, sorting based on users' willingness to pay and delivering top software opportunities to "Pro" members.

Ceptize's development will not stop here. After having enough members, Ceptize will add voting system which help users validating willingness to pay of opportunities. Accompany to voting system, an OSINT is built to scrape evidences of willingness to pay and other research information. At that time, Ceptize can become an "effective demand discovery and aggression system".
