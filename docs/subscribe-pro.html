About Us

Solve best features which users are willing to pay for.

Software businesses need to ensure that users are willing to and/or ready to pay for softwares' features before they can invest resources to build those features. The Build-Measure-Learn cycle has become popular. Which features will be next MVP? Is it worth to build it? Ceptize are trying to solve this problem. Ceptize are extracting "willing to pay" features from most interested products, sorting based on users' willingness to pay and delivering top software opportunities to "Pro" members.

Ceptize's development will not stop here. After having enough members, Ceptize will add voting system which help users validating willingness to pay of opportunities. Accompany to voting system, an OSINT is built to scrape evidences of willingness to pay and other research information. At that time, Ceptize can become an "effective demand discovery and aggression system".
